# AGI Made This Movie — cohost brief (WuBuDesk as host/narrator)

Boss's open-source film project. The cohost is its **host + narrator** on
stream. This doc captures the shape so WuBuDesk is launch-ready the day it
drops. (Visual vibe still being absorbed from 9 preview clips in
cache/videos/ — this brief is from the boss's verbal direction + will be
refined once the frames are viewed.)

## THE SHAPE (boss's words)
- **Core:** an extremely long **~1.5-hour film** made by the AGI.
- **Rollout:** a bunch of **TikTok clips** released alongside/after:
  - some are **text** clips (on-screen words)
  - some are **music** clips
  - some are full **music videos** — the movie has a **MUSICAL element**
    (songs / music videos are part of the work, not just a score).
- Intent: "get the vibe across" — it's promotional + artistic, not a dry
  technical demo.

## WUBODESK'S ROLE
- **Host/narrator** of the project on stream: introduces it ("an AGI made a
  movie — here's how"), frames the musical element, walks chat through the
  clip rollout.
- **Honest receipts:** explains what the AGI did vs. what the human did;
  surfaces the repo/commits as proof it was AGI-made. No overclaim.
- **Clip companion:** when a TikTok text/music/music-video clip is on screen,
  the cohost's ticker + mood react (text clip = neutral/read; music = cyan
  pulse; music video = bright, animated).

## THE VISUAL LANGUAGE (absorbed from 9 preview clips, 2026-08-04)
- **WuBu wizard universe.** Robed Orders in blue robes with white **"W"/"B"**
  sigils and "WuBu" markings; pointed-hat wizards with **glowing-orb staffs**.
- **Color grade:** dark **blue + green** base, **gold/amber trim** on robes and
  staff orbs. Mysterious, somber, ritual. (Homage-heavy — the "Wu" even
  resonates Wu-Tang; it's outsider-art / DIY AGI cinema.)
- **Two layers:** (1) the *magical* — solemn gatherings, ceremonies, orbs;
  (2) the *real-world self-aware* — wizard cosplay in a retail store, "WuBu"
  hoodie, first-person REC-cam gameplay footage with "WuBu" yellow caution
  tape and bust-filled "altar" rooms. Playful + eerie.
- **No on-screen text/typography** in the narrative frames (text lives in the
  TikTok *text clips*, separate from the film). Music/music-video element is
  part of the release, not always in-frame.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE COHOST
- The **wizard metaphor is LITERAL** in the film → the all-seeing-eye + sigil
  overlay is thematically dead-on. Add **wizard-hat / W-B sigil** accent to
  movie mode to echo the Order's iconography.
- Palette: keep green/cyan base, add **deep blue** + **gold/amber** (movie mode
  already amber). The cohost should feel like the **host of a magical Order's
  broadcast**.
- When a **text clip** is on screen → cohost reads it (neutral). **Music /
  music-video clip** → cohost goes musical (cyan pulse / bright, animated).
- Tone: reverent about the craft ("an AGI made a whole wizard mythos + the
  music"), playful about the real-world layer ("and then it cosplays at the
  mall"). Honest: AGI-made, open-source, commit-trailed.
